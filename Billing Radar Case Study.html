Finding · updated this scan

Salesforce still can't answer "what stage is this at" — and the scoping filter has its own blind spot

You said checking SFDC takes a lot of back and forth. The core problem from the last scan still holds: of the 180 regional Order records verified 15 Oct, Sync Status read "Pending" on 176 and blank on 4, and Invoice Date, Total Invoice Amount and Balance Due were null on 100%. That pattern just broke for the first time: Marlowe Institute's September run (INV-8390) now reads Sync Status: Synced instead of Pending — the only record, ever, to hold anything but Pending or blank. Worth flagging to whoever owns the write-back as a sign something changed, even though the amount/date fields are still untouched.

New this scan — the R1/R2/R3 filter itself has a gap. Every scan so far, including this one's first pass, scoped Salesforce Orders to Billing_Country_Code__c IN ('R1','R2','R3'). That field is null on Northfield Retail Co.'s INV-8841 — a real regional order, billed to h.lim@northfieldretail.example — so it was excluded from every prior pull, Teams-sourced or not, and sat unseen with Finance for nearly two weeks. A wider check found the same null Billing Country Code on several other plainly-regional orders (Fernwood Polytechnic, Veridian University, Metro Redevelopment Authority, Cendra, Harborview Welfare Services, Caldwell University), though those carry "PUB-" project codes from the separate self-service public-programme system Mira's team doesn't manually handle, so they're out of scope for this radar rather than missed billing items. Northfield's "EC-" code is the one that matters here, because EC- is exactly the manual Priya→Devin→Finance workflow this board tracks.

What follows from this: the SLA/finding framework still holds and Teams is still the ledger, as below. But until the Billing Country Code field is reliably populated, this radar can't trust the Salesforce filter alone to know it has seen every live regional order — worth a note to the SFDC admin alongside the Sync Status / Invoice Date ask from before.

The fix · no new system, no new tracker

One thread per invoice, one word per handoff

Your team already posts everything needed. What's missing is that it lands in three different chats, in prose, with no consistent key — so state can only be reassembled by a human reading backwards. Two small changes make it readable automatically.

Change one: one thread per invoice, in Sales & Ops only. Opened by whoever requests billing, titled to a fixed shape:

[BILL] INV-8050 · EC-551884 · Kestrel Environmental Authority · Vendors@Gov

Every later message about that invoice is a reply in that thread. Finance Coordination stays for finance-wide topics; your 1:1s stay for thinking out loud. Billing state lives in exactly one place.

Change two: each function starts its reply with one keyword. Nobody types more than they already do, and nobody has to spell your name — I key off the INV number, not a mention. "Mirael", "Mirabel", or no mention at all makes no difference.

My side of it

What runs automatically

  1. 08:00 APAC time each weekday, I read the threads. All three venues, keyed on INV and EC codes, cross-checked against SFDC Order Status for anything that has quietly gone to Paid.
  2. I rebuild this board and update it in place. Same link, always current. Nobody types a status into a tracker.
  3. Mira gets a short message — only what needs her. Not the whole pipeline. "INV-8841 is 2 weeks with Finance and nobody's replied" is the entire message. If nothing needs her, she hears nothing.
  4. Every breach arrives as a drafted nudge. Addressed to the right person, in the right thread, with the timestamps already in it. She reads it and sends it, or does not.
  5. When sales come down hard, the answer is already written. Timestamped, per step, per owner. No investigation, no four places to check, no defending the timeline from memory.

Until the convention is adopted, this still works — just less precisely. I can already reconstruct everything on this page from prose messages. The keywords make it exact and let me tell a stall apart from a silence.
